Advocate-on-Record (AOR) Gateway
For Outstation Briefing Counsel

Dedicated Supreme Court registry filing, SLP procedural curation, urgent caveat lodging, and appearance desk for advocates practicing across High Courts of India (Allahabad, Bombay, Calcutta, Punjab & Haryana, Patna, etc.).

SLP Vetting & Registry Filing

Rigorous compliance with Supreme Court Rules 2013, limitation reckoning, certificate of fitness curation, and certified e-filing before the Registry.

Immediate Caveat Lodging

Expedited caveat lodging under Section 148A CPC across all court benches to guarantee right of audience and protect clients against ex-parte adverse orders.

Urgent Listing & Mentioning

Procedural curation for urgent mentioning slips before the Hon’ble Chief Justice of India Court and vacation benches for interim stay applications.

Co-Counsel & Senior Briefing

Seamless collaboration with arguing Senior Advocates or state briefing counsels via hybrid in-person chambers conferences and digital case dossiers.

01

Impugned Order & Limitation Reckoning

Certified or certified-web copy of the High Court / Appellate Tribunal order, with date of judgment for statutory 90-day limitation computation.

02

Pleadings & Lower Court Records

Complete sequence of pleadings before High Court, Trial Court chargesheets/plaints, arbitral award transcripts, and marked exhibits.

03

Official English Translations

Under Order VIII Rule 2 of Supreme Court Rules, true certified translations of all regional language vernacular documents and annexures.

04

Signed Vakalatnama & Affidavit Sets

Original executed Vakalatnama authorizing Advocate-on-Record (AOR), supported by non-judicial stamp affidavits verified by Oath Commissioner.
